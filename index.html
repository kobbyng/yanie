Made with Both hands

I started Yanie from my kitchen in Abuja, Blending oils and testing reeds on weekends after work. i wanted a scent product that felt Nigerian, not an imported candle that smells like a vermont winter.
Every bottle is blended, filled and labelled by hand. i source our reeds locally and partner with a Lagos based glass supplier. This is a small business, and i'm proud of it.

HOW IT WORKS

Set up in minutes

No wax, no flame, no fuss. Reed diffusers work quietly releasing fragrance through natural evaporation, hour after hour.

01 Unbox

Your Yanie set arrives wrapped, bottled, with reeds, and care card.

02 Fill & Place

Pour the oil into the bottle, insert the reeds, and place it in your favorite spot.

03 Insert Reeds

Place all reeds in. Flip them after 24 hours for a stronger first scent.

04 Enjoy

One bottle lasts 6-8 weeks depending on usage.
